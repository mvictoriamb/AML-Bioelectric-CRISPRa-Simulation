DATA ATTRIBUTION

Public source data: cBioPortal Datahub, TCGA-LAML and Beat AML/OHSU 2018.
Pinned Datahub revision: e1b75cd25bf7436d16719b7d76117ad8f3f66e54.
Retrieved: 1 November 2026.

The expression values and public specimen identifiers in
data/selected_samples_expression.csv are an extraction from these providers.
The two-probe AML discovery subset in data/discovery_aml_pair.csv is extracted
from the project's local GSE9476 expression matrix without changing values.
It is not a newly normalized reconstruction of the GEO raw data.

The repository's software licence does not replace the source data terms.
cBioPortal Datahub describes its database under ODbL and describes TCGA data
under the Broad Institute GDAC TCGA Analysis Pipeline terms. Preserve source
attribution and applicable terms when redistributing extracted data:
https://github.com/cBioPortal/datahub#license
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Primary study references:
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1301689
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0623-z

SHA256 checksums of the downloaded inputs are included in results/results.json.
The large original matrices are not included in the upload bundle.
